Put your own Fear Effect 2: Retro Helix (USA, v1.1) disc images here: all four discs, BIN/CUE or raw .bin/.img.
Any file names work: install.bat recognises the discs by their content (serials SLUS-01266, SLUS-01275,
SLUS-01276, SLUS-01277). The installer adds small .cue files of its own here; your files are never changed.
