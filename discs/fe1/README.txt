Put your own Fear Effect (USA) disc images here: all four discs, BIN/CUE or raw .bin/.img.
Any file names work: install.bat recognises the discs by their content (serials SLUS-00920, SLUS-01056,
SLUS-01057, SLUS-01058). The installer adds small .cue files of its own here; your files are never changed.
